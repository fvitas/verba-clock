Verba Clock — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 27, 2026

Verba Clock does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. There is nothing more to this policy, but here it is spelled out.

No data collection

The app collects no personal information, no usage analytics, no crash reports, no identifiers, and no diagnostics of any kind.

No network access

Verba Clock is fully offline. It makes no network requests — there is no server, no third-party SDK, no advertising, and no tracking. On Android, the app does not request the internet permission at all.

Your settings stay on your device

Your preferences (theme, language, display options) are stored only on your device so the app, its widgets, and the watch app can share them. They never leave your device.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published at this address with an updated date.
